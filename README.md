# Customer-Churn-Analysis
Customer Churn Analysis for a Telecommunication Provider (U.S. based telecommunications provider examples: Verizon, Comcast, AT&T, etc.). 

I perform customer churn analysis for a U.S. based telecommunications giant, identifying trends and patterns amongst the largest churning consumer groups by gender, age group, geography, services used, payment method, contract length, tenure, and internet service. The analysis also highlight the reasons for customer churn and implements a Random Forest to predict churn and identify high risk customers.  Insights are then synthesized into a Power BI dashboard

## Brief Description:

* The analysis spanned across 6,418 customers.  Of these 411 were new joiners, whereas 1,732 (27%) had churned.
* Churn Rate = 27%.  The time duration of this dataset is kept unclear due to confidentiality purposes
* Churn Rate by Gender: Females (64.15%) Males (35.85%).
* Churn Rate by Reason: Competitor (44%)
* Churn Rate by Tenure: >= 24 Months (28%)
* Churn Rate by Contract and Internet Type, respectively: Month-to-Month (47%) and Fiber Optic (41%)
* Churn Rate by State: TK

## Female Churners:

* Females older than 50 churn at a rate of 32% compared to 24% for those aged 40-50.
* Females who churned typically had a greater than 24 month tenure and were on the month-to-month contract.  These women mostly subscribed to Unlimited Data (67%), Phone Service (90.9%), Paperless Billing (59.5%), and Internet Service (77.7%).  This consumer group largely subscribed to the Fiber Optic (42.5%) internet.  The churners were based in Jammu & Kashmir (59%), like the rest of the churners (53.7%)
* We lost 45% of the female customer base to the competitors for the following reasons:
     * Competitors made better offers (37%)
     * Compeitors had better devices (36%)
     * Competitors offered higher download speeds (14%)
     * Competitors offered more data (13.3%)
     
## Male Churners:

* Like females, males over 50 were 30.4% times likely churn than those aged 40-50 (22%)
* Of the males churning, 26% had an over 24 month tenure with the company and were found to have a month-to-month contract (46.6%).  They were also largely based in Jammu and Kashmir (53.7%), used Fiber Optic (38.5%) internet, and subscribed to Unlimited Data (68.4%), Phone Service (89.2%), and Internet Service (79.4%).
* We lost 43% of the male customer base to competitors for the following reasons:
     * Compeitors had better devices (38%)
     * Competitors made better offers (36%)
     * Competitors offered more data (14%)
     * Competitors offered higher download speeds (12%)
